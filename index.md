    <section class="class-rhythm" aria-labelledby="class-rhythm-heading">
      <h3 id="class-rhythm-heading">How the learning cycle works</h3>
      <ol class="flow">
        <li><span>Begin with a Korean Studies question and identify the texts that could answer it.</span></li>
        <li><span>Learn the analytical idea and the assumptions behind it.</span></li>
        <li><span>Build the workflow in Orange, then reinforce core concepts through guided R practice.</span></li>
        <li><span>Inspect the output against real source texts instead of treating a model as the answer.</span></li>
        <li><span>Save, document, and share the work so another person can understand what you did.</span></li>
      </ol>
    </section>
